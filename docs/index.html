Gesture Guide

1 Draw

Raise your index finger only. Move your hand to draw on the canvas.

2 Select

Raise your index and middle fingers. Move to the toolbar to choose a tool, color, or brush size.

3 Zoom

Raise your index, middle, and ring fingers. Change the distance between your thumb and index finger to zoom.
