✋Gesture Guide

1

Draw

Raise your index finger only. Move your hand to draw on the canvas.

2

Select

Raise your index and middle fingers. Move to the toolbar and hold over a tool, color or size for a moment to choose it.

3

Zoom

Raise your index, middle, and ring fingers. Change the distance between your thumb and index finger to zoom.

4

Rotate in 3D

In 3D view, point with your index finger and move it to spin the model. The 3-finger zoom works there too.
